A modern, interactive Tableau dashboard designed to uncover sales trends, profitability patterns, and top‑performing customers by comparing key metrics.

---

## 📖 Project Overview

This project visualises business performance using two core dashboard pages:

- **Page 1 – Sales & Profit Overview**  
  KPI cards for highest/lowest months, subcategory profit breakdown, a regional sales heatmap, and a time‑series trend chart.

- **Page 2 – Customer Deep‑Dive**  
  Monthly comparison KPIs and a ranked table of the **Top 10 Customers by Profit**, complete with last order dates and order counts.

All dashboards are built with a clean, professional layout suitable for executive reporting.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

🏆 **Year‑over‑Year KPIs**  
Instantly compare highest and lowest performing months between 2017 and 2016.

📈 **Sales & Profit by Subcategory**  
A dual‑axis chart revealing which product subcategories drive revenue and which deliver the best margins.

🌍 **Regional Heatmap**  
Cross‑reference subcategory sales across geographic regions to spot territory‑specific strengths.

⏳ **Trend Analysis**  
Interactive line chart tracking sales and profit momentum over time.

👥 **Top 10 Profit Customers**  
Ranked table including customer name, last order date, profit, total sales, and number of orders – essential for targeted retention strategies.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Seth Vernon** is the most profitable customer, generating **$939** in profit from 4 orders.
- The highest‑grossing month in 2017 surpassed the 2016 peak, indicating strong year‑on‑year growth.
- Certain subcategories show high sales but low profit margins, flagging potential cost‑optimisation opportunities.
- Regional heatmaps highlight areas where targeted marketing could boost underperforming subcategories.
